## About the Project

### **Plant watering can** - It is a 3D printed Plant watering Can Design in Fusion 360. 
#### Used in Garden and Indore Plants
### **plant Collector** - It is a 3D printed Holder For indoor plant And having A tray to collect water and Separate plant array.
#### Can be used in Home and office

## why i made this?
### I built this Plant watering can cause I needed some small watering can that I used to Watering my indoor plants. 
### I built this water plant holder for my home plants. (Indoor Plants). It's not possible to daily Drain the plant. Take the whole pot outside Sunlight. So I make this. Which have separate plant array Which is very easy to take out and It also have Water draining tray.
